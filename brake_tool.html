The honest deal

No hype. The Brake is a trade, and here is exactly what you're trading. If a page like this promises you upside with no cost, close it.

What you give up

  • Some upside in raging bull markets — you re-enter a bit late after a bottom.
  • The occasional whipsaw: choppy, trendless years can cost a few percent in false signals.
  • Bragging rights. You will not out-return a lucky buy-and-holder in a good year.

What you get

  • Drawdowns cut roughly in half — the difference between "annoying" and "un-holdable."
  • You sat in cash through the worst crashes instead of riding them to the floor.
  • A rule that acts when you'd freeze — no willpower, no doom-scrolling, no panic-sell.
